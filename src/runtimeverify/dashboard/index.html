SPRT Formal Specification

Cumulative LLR Equation:

$$S_n = \sum_{i=1}^n \ln \left(\frac{P(x_i \mid H_1)}{P(x_i \mid H_0)}\right)$$

Wald Boundary Formulations:
• $A \approx \ln\left(\frac{1 - \beta}{\alpha}\right) = \ln(0.95 / 0.05) \approx +2.944$
• $B \approx \ln\left(\frac{\beta}{1 - \alpha}\right) = \ln(0.05 / 0.95) \approx -2.944$
Optimal Stopping Time (Wald's Lemma):

Minimizes the expected sample size $E[N]$ among all tests with significance $\alpha$ and power $1 - \beta$. Early exits trigger on the very first anomalous sequence.
